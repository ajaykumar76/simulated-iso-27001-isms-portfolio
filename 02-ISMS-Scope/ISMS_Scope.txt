# MedSecure Cloud Ltd. — ISMS Scope Statement

**Document ID:** MSC-GRC-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Simulated Portfolio Project  
**Date:** 10 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the proposed scope of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for MedSecure Cloud Ltd. as part of a simulated ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation project.

## 2. Proposed Scope

The proposed ISMS covers the design, development, operation, maintenance and support of the MedSecure cloud-based clinic management platform, including the people, processes, information and technology used to deliver the service.

The scope includes:

- The production web application and its supporting cloud infrastructure.
- Databases containing patient and customer information.
- Cloud-based file storage and backups.
- Source-code repositories and software development activities.
- Identity and access management for employees and administrators.
- Employee endpoints used to administer or develop the platform.
- Security monitoring, incident management and vulnerability management.
- Relevant third-party services and suppliers supporting the platform.
- Business processes supporting service availability and information security.

## 3. Organizational Boundary

The proposed scope covers the employees and functions responsible for developing, operating, securing and supporting the platform.

Relevant management, IT, security, development and customer-support responsibilities are included where they affect the security of the service.

## 4. Physical and Technological Boundaries

The proposed scope includes the Malta-based business operations and the cloud-hosted systems used to deliver the platform.

The cloud provider's underlying infrastructure is operated by the provider. MedSecure remains responsible for identifying and managing its own security responsibilities under the applicable cloud service agreements.

## 5. Interfaces and Dependencies

The ISMS depends on relevant external parties and services, potentially including:

- Microsoft Azure or another contracted cloud provider.
- Email and productivity services.
- External software and technology suppliers.
- Customer organizations accessing the platform.
- Internet and telecommunications providers.

These dependencies must be confirmed during the project.

## 6. Proposed Exclusions

No critical platform component is excluded from the proposed scope at this stage.

Any future exclusion must be documented, justified and assessed for its effect on information security and the integrity of the ISMS.

## 7. Assumptions and Outstanding Validation

The organizational structure, technology environment, locations and business processes described in this portfolio are fictional assumptions.

In a real implementation, management would validate the scope against the organization's actual activities, interested-party requirements, interfaces, dependencies and applicable legal or contractual obligations.
