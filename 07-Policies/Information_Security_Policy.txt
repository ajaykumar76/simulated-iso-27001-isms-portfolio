1. Purpose

This policy establishes the proposed information security principles and responsibilities for MedSecure Cloud Ltd., a fictional Malta-based healthcare software provider. It aims to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of company information, patient-related information and supporting systems.

2. Scope

This policy is proposed to apply to employees, contractors, relevant suppliers and other authorized users who access MedSecure Cloud Ltd. information or systems within the proposed ISMS scope.

The detailed ISMS boundaries are documented separately in the ISMS Scope document.

3. Policy Principles

MedSecure Cloud Ltd. proposes to:

Protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss and destruction.

Identify and assess information security risks using documented criteria.

Assign responsibility for information security decisions and actions.

Grant access according to business need and least privilege.

Protect authentication credentials and use multi-factor authentication where required by the approved security standard.

Maintain appropriate asset inventories and information classifications.

Assess relevant third-party and cloud service risks.

Manage vulnerabilities, system changes and security configurations.

Maintain suitable logging, monitoring and incident response arrangements.

Establish backup, recovery and business continuity arrangements appropriate to identified risks.

Provide relevant information security awareness and training.

Evaluate compliance obligations and retain appropriate evidence.

Review information security performance and improve the proposed ISMS.

4. Roles and Responsibilities

Top Management: Proposed responsibility for approving the policy, assigning resources and reviewing significant information security risks.

GRC Owner: Proposed responsibility for coordinating risk assessments, maintaining ISMS documentation and monitoring compliance activities.

IT Manager: Proposed responsibility for access administration, system security configurations, asset management and backup arrangements.

Development Lead: Proposed responsibility for secure development, vulnerability remediation and controlled production changes.

All Personnel: Expected to follow approved policies, protect credentials, complete required training and report suspected security incidents.

These role assignments are proposed for the simulation and have not been formally approved.

5. Risk Management

Information security risks should be identified, analyzed and evaluated using the documented risk assessment methodology. Risk treatment actions should have assigned owners, target dates and verification evidence.

Residual risks requiring acceptance must be presented to an appropriately authorized decision-maker. Risk acceptance must be recorded and must not be assumed merely because a treatment action has been planned.

6. Compliance and Incident Reporting

Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and customer requirements should be identified and periodically reviewed.

Suspected information security incidents should be reported promptly through a designated reporting route. Incidents should be assessed, documented, investigated and managed according to an approved incident response procedure.

7. Exceptions

Exceptions to approved information security requirements should be documented, justified, risk-assessed and approved by an authorized person. Appropriate safeguards and review dates should be recorded.

8. Monitoring and Review

Compliance with this policy should be evaluated through appropriate reviews, evidence checks, internal audit activities and management review.

The policy should be reviewed at least annually and when significant changes occur to the business, technology, risks or applicable requirements.
