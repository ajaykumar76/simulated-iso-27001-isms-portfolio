# MedSecure Cloud Ltd. — Company Profile

**Document ID:** MSC-GRC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Document Status:** Draft — Simulated Portfolio Project  
**Date:** 10 October 2026

## 1. Company Overview

MedSecure Cloud Ltd. is a fictional Malta-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider that develops and operates a cloud-based clinic management platform for private medical clinics.

The platform supports appointment scheduling, patient information management, document storage, billing and administrative reporting.

## 2. Organization Details

- **Location:** Malta
- **Industry:** Healthcare technology / SaaS
- **Employees:** 35 (assumed for this simulation)
- **Customers:** Private medical clinics
- **Cloud environment:** Microsoft Azure (assumed)
- **IT team:** 5 employees (assumed)
- **Security team:** 2 employees (assumed)
- **Development team:** 8 employees (assumed)

These figures are fictional assumptions for the portfolio exercise, not verified facts about a real organization.

## 3. Information Assets

The company processes or manages the following information and technology assets:

- Patient information and medical documents
- Customer and clinic account information
- Employee records
- Billing and financial records
- Application source code
- Cloud infrastructure and databases
- User accounts and authentication data
- Backups and security logs

## 4. Business Objectives

1. Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer information.
2. Maintain reliable access to the clinic management platform.
3. Identify and treat information security risks.
4. Define security responsibilities and accountability.
5. Evaluate relevant legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.
6. Establish and continually improve an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

## 5. Project Objective

The objective of this simulated project is to design an ISO/IEC 27001:2022-aligned ISMS for MedSecure Cloud Ltd.

The project will cover organizational context, ISMS scope, asset identification, information security risk assessment, risk treatment, control applicability, documented policies, internal audit and corrective actions.

## 6. Important Disclaimer

MedSecure Cloud Ltd. is fictional. This document represents a learning and portfolio exercise only. No real audit has been performed, no actual controls have been verified, and no ISO certification is claimed.
