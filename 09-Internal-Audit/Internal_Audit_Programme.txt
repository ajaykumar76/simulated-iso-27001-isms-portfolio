1. Objective

Define a proposed internal audit programme for evaluating whether the fictional organization's planned ISMS documentation and selected information security controls meet defined requirements and are supported by appropriate evidence.

2. Audit Scope

The proposed audit covers:

ISMS context and scope

Asset inventory and information classification

Risk assessment and risk treatment

Statement of Applicability

Information security and access control policies

Access reviews and privileged access

Evidence collection and retention

Incident response, logging, backup, and vulnerability management documentation

Corrective actions and management review

3. Audit Criteria

The proposed criteria include:

Applicable requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022

The organization's approved policies and procedures, once approved

Applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations, once validated

Documented risk assessment and treatment criteria

The full standard should be consulted for formal audit work.

4. Audit Approach

Confirm the audit scope, criteria, and objectives.

Review relevant documents and available evidence.

Interview responsible personnel where applicable.

Test a suitable sample of controls where authorization and access permit.

Record objective evidence and the basis for each conclusion.

Document findings against specific requirements.

Assign corrective actions where justified.

Follow up and verify action effectiveness.

5. Independence and Objectivity

Auditors should be objective and impartial. Where practical, an auditor should not audit their own work. Audit activities should be conducted by competent individuals who are sufficiently independent of the activities being examined.
