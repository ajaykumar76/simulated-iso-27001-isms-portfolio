## 4. Verification required before relying on the acceptance

The GRC analyst should obtain and verify:

1. **Identity and authority:** the executive's identity, role, documented delegation and authority to accept Critical risk.
2. **Decision record:** dated, attributable approval that identifies the specific risk and the rationale for accepting it.
3. **Scope:** affected assets, services, data, processes and relevant threat scenarios.
4. **Risk basis:** the assessment method, likelihood, impact, residual-risk rationale and applicable risk-tolerance threshold.
5. **Conditions:** interim safeguards, monitoring, escalation triggers, accountable owners and funding or treatment commitments.
6. **Time limit:** an expiry or review date and the event that would trigger an earlier reassessment.
7. **Mandatory obligations:** confirmation that acceptance does not purport to waive legal, regulatory, contractual or other mandatory requirements.
8. **Communication:** evidence that relevant risk owners and control owners have been informed.

A statement that an executive has accepted the risk is not, by itself, sufficient evidence that all these requirements have been met.
